# Domain glossary

- **Ticket:** One source support record identified by TICKET_ID. The sample contains twelve records.
- **Customer:** A person represented in the source queue. Maya has two visible tickets; there are eleven distinct customer names.
- **Priority:** The order of attention justified by deadlines, unanswered replies, broken commitments, and dependencies.
- **Reply deadline:** The date by which support owes a response to the latest recorded customer message under the stated business-day assumption.
- **Status:** The operator's recorded position in the support workflow. A status change does not itself perform an external action.
- **Internal note:** An operator's saved annotation. It is not a customer reply or evidence of a payment, shipment, or claim filing.
- **Resolved:** The operator records that the case is settled and supplies a resolution note. This does not independently prove an external outcome.
- **Related case:** Another ticket linked by a stated common concern or symptom. Linking preserves every source record.
- **Candidate firmware issue:** A source-supported symptom pattern that needs engineering investigation; it is not a confirmed diagnosis.
- **Refund eligibility:** Whether the request meets policy or an authorized exception. This is separate from permission to pay before warehouse confirmation.
- **Engineering issue:** A GitHub task for building or verifying the application. It is separate from a customer ticket.
- **Verified:** The current implementation meets its acceptance criteria with recorded evidence. It is not a customer-ticket status.
